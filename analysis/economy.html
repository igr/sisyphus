      • Each weight is out of 500: a joker of weight w is w in 500 of the jokers by the path. The chance on his card is 500 ÷ w, rounded.
      • After each joker, the next stands 72 to 144 turns of walking ahead: 1.5 game days on average. So a joker of weight w comes about once in 750 ÷ w game days.
      • A line is a joke or a riddle of one or two texts. A joker tells his lines in turn, and has at least as many lines as his weight: so each joker comes back to a line after some 750 game days. A joker of 1 in 100 or rarer is rare: his card has a gold banner.
