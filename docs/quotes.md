# Wise words

The quote panel under the display shows one saying at a time, in a new random order in each round. `QUOTES` in `game/Quotes.kt` holds them: real sayings, and the words of Sisyphus and his world.

## Rules for a new saying

- Take it from the text itself, in a translation published before 1929, or from an English text written before 1929. These are in the public domain. Quote websites often give wrong authors.
- You may cut it to one sentence or clause, drop a leading "And" or "But", change old forms (thou, hath, -eth) to modern ones, and use American spelling. Do not change other words.
- It must stand alone, and keep the calm mood of the game.
- Only the characters of `PixelFont` can show. On the smallest phone, the text must fit in 3 lines, and "— author" in 1 line. `QuotesTest` checks both.
