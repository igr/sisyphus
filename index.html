Sisyphus

Turn the knob. Push the rock.

A small, calm pixel-art game for iPhone and Android. Turn the bronze knob, and Sisyphus pushes his boulder up an endless Greek hill. Time moves only while he works: the sun rises over the sea, crosses the sky and sets behind the hill. Wise words appear as you climb.

  • Days pass only when you push. Each day has its own weather.
  • Almost 150 wise words, from Camus and Heraclitus to the Rock itself.
  • Clicks and haptics with every turn of the knob.
  • No ads, no accounts, no data collected.
