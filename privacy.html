Privacy Policy

Sisyphus for iPhone · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Short version

Sisyphus does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app keeps

The app saves your game progress on your device only: the day, the time of day, your place on the hill, the knob position, the current quote and the sound setting. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network

The app does not connect to the internet.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
